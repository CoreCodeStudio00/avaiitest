Our Story

More Than a
Meal — An Experience

Avaii brings together the best of international cuisine in one beautifully designed space overlooking Beit Meri.

9AM Until 1AM

Nestled in the hills of Beit Meri, Avaii Resto-Cafe is a space that celebrates good food, beautiful design, and the perfect mood. Whether you're joining us for a leisurely breakfast, a business lunch, or an evening out with friends — we've crafted every detail to make it memorable.

With a menu spanning international flavors — from fresh salads and gourmet burgers to fine pasta, premium steaks, and craft cocktails — there's always something new to discover at Avaii.
